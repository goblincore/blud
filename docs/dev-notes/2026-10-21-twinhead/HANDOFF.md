## The machine twin's demon skull (how it was made — redo from the session .blend if needed)

From the owner's `~/Downloads/skeleton-demon-mesh.glb` (one connected mesh, skull fused to spine):
voxel remesh the WHOLE mesh at 2.5 mm (cropping first opens holes and the remesh collapses), then EXACT booleans:
keep box x -0.23..0.37, y -0.95..-0.40, z 0.53..1.0; subtract a side-profile prism
`[(-0.612,.40),(-0.612,.668),(-0.645,.672),(-0.645,.738),(-0.30,.738),(-0.30,.40)]` (ref coords, y=-front);
clavicle-tip boxes |x-0.07|>~0.12 below z 0.685; slanted ellipsoid eye sockets at (0.013,-0.800,0.753) and
(0.125,-0.788,0.751), radii (0.032, 0.048 deep, 0.021), rotated +-20 deg about y. The owner prefers this cut (keeps the
FULL jaw + chin point) over tighter cuts — do not cut the jaw bottom. Then decimate to ~16k faces, colours = nearest
sculpt texel ('Baked_BaseColor' image) x Cycles AO bake, seat at 0.80 onto blob (0.18, 2.056, 0.17), pitch 12 deg
face-up, lift +0.06. A scratch copy WITH the reference is at the session scratchpad `session-with-ref.blend` (not in git).

- Eyes: `eyeglow` material (kit-overlay LOOK, emissive 7). The light they throw is a baked `_glow` vertex attribute on
  the skull (falloff/facing/occlusion from each ember) -> `GLOW_FROM_ATTRIBUTE` in `webgpu/kit-overlay.ts` makes the
  `skullmetal` material a node material with red emission. No real lights (would rebuild pipelines).
- Gap under the skull: machine flesh neck is now a stump (end y 1.893); a 9-cable twisted bundle + collar + brass ring
  run from y 1.875 up into the skull base (underside behind the jaw is at y ~2.045). Reviewed from behind: fills the gap.

## Left pauldron (`clavicle.l|pauldron`)

Generated by `assets-source/twinhead-kit/make_pauldron.py` (run inside Blender: `exec(open(path).read())`). Parametric
ellipsoid shells round the shoulder ball (blob (0.316, 1.769, 0.138), r 0.14), tilted 32 deg outward: a cranium-like cap
with swept front/back points and two cascading lames closing to slivers, rolled lips, sagittal crest, rib ridges, three
raked horn spines, knobs; voxel remesh 2 mm, smooth, clouds noise, zigzag sutures pressed in (vertex push: an EXACT
boolean collapsed the mesh), decimated to ~14k tris, `skullmetal` with a blurred Cycles AO bake (0.92 * ao^2.2) as
vertex colours. Replaced the three box plates + bolts. Tune shape at the `capC`/`A, B, Cc` and lame lines.
