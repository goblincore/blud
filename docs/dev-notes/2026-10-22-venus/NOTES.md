# Venus: a heavy biomech woman (2026-10-22)

Owner brief: a female biomechanical enemy, "more fleshy... the cyberbride proved too thin and waifish". The result is
a big, heavy, curvy, full-figured body with wide hips, thick thighs and a full rear. It is a horror enemy with grafts,
not a pin-up. The owner liked the cyberbride's hair and face (abandoned PR goblincore/blud#5), so her head is ported.

`?character=venus` in the lab, `?spawn=venus` in the game.

## How it went

1. **The first block-out read as a stocky man.** Every mass was big, and the chest, waist, belly and rear all reached
   ~0.2 m. Contrast fixed it: hips 0.275 half-width against a 0.17 waist, and the rear 11 cm behind the waist. See
   biomech-style.md, "Heavy, full-figured bodies".
2. **The first turntable was another worktree's lab.** `blob:shot` reused a Vite on port 5233 served from a sibling
   worktree, which rendered a fallback body. Use your own `LAB_VITE_PORT`/`LAB_CDP_PORT`.
3. **The eyes were inherited.** Rendering the original cyberbride showed the same fault: the glow prims sat 1.5 cm
   above the painted sockets and 2 cm proud. The nose also drooped over the maw. Both are fixed here.
4. **Kit sides.** On down-pointing bones `BoneFrame.s` points inward, so the thigh plates first landed between her
   legs.
