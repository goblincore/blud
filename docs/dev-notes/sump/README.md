## Faces (round 2 — after owner review said the first pass was invisible)

- **Main face**: a hand-authored ALPHA-MASKED decal worn at MULTIPLY
  (`sheet image sump-face.png`, `decal 0`), carrying the value contrast the
  same-colour geometry cannot: brow shadow, dark eye pits with a pale glint
  seated on the authored eyeballs, crushed-nose flanks, nasolabial folds, a
  crooked mouth line, and the sagging -x cheek. Positions are derived in the
  cranium hs frame inside the generator; `projScale 0.19/0.22`,
  `projCentre 0.50/0.47`. The sheet's glow is gated off
  (`eyeGlowCut 0.99 + eyeGlowAmp 0`) — the default glow painted two red
  zombie eyes on the first turntable. The decal's neutral base is
  normalised to the measured mean (0.494), so the base is a no-op and only
  the features deviate.
- **Head pitch**: the first skeleton aimed the face at the floor (skull
  40°); it now stops at 26° so the face addresses a player's eye height
  while still hanging forward out of the raised back.
- **Second face** (torso cluster over the overgrown arm): enlarged dark
  crater + pale glossy eyeball at the socket rim, heavy brow ledge, two
  stepped grooves and a small dark aperture so the fold reads as a slightly
  parted mouth. The face sheet only projects in head space, so this one is
  geometry-only by construction.

## Design decisions (short form; the .blob header carries the full set)

- ~2.2 m crown on a hip line of 1.05: short load-bearing legs, the hunch is
  the crouch. `pitch` is per-bone absolute (not accumulated) — the first
  draft leaned only 24° total and read upright.
- Belly carried FORWARD (z 0.19) of the thighs so it hangs between the hips
  without swallowing them; belly bottom ~0.73-0.78 vs knee 0.555.
- Asymmetric arms authored with `side=l` / `side=r` flesh prims over a
  symmetric rig: the +x arm is a knotted overgrown limb whose fist hangs at
  ~0.69-0.75 (knee 0.555); the -x arm stays human with one misshapen bulge.
- Eyes (both faces) are non-glowing; nothing on the body is emissive
  (pinned as zero glow prims in the test).
- Palette: waxy grey-beige base, bruised-plum mottle, raw red only in
  `deepColor`/mouth/seam; wetness 0.15 with gloss reserved to painted prims.

## Known defects and limitations (honest list)

1. `blob:render-check sump` FAILS: GPU-only hole clusters, worst 16 px
   across (threshold 12), owners scattered over anisotropic prims (fist,
   shins). The shipped bloatmaw fails the same check (worst 12 px) and
   gnasher passes at 8 px, so this is the known occluder-hull class
   (`webgpu/occluder-hull.ts`), pre-existing, aggravated slightly by sump's
   larger anisotropic masses. An authoring-side anisotropy tweak was tried
   and reverted (it moved the mismatch without shrinking it). The PRD
   forbids renderer changes in this task; the owner should treat this as
   renderer work, not character work.
2. The second face reads as a heavy-lidded dark eye + brow + parted fold at
   inspection distance; at gameplay distance it is still more "wound on the
   shoulder" than "second face". Three relief rounds are recorded in the
   .blob comments; the face sheet cannot help here (head-space projection
   only), so further work is geometry: larger crater, stronger rim shadow.
3. The main face reads best from the front three-quarter at or below eye
   level; off-axis it falls back to the geometry relief (brow/nose/jaw).
4. `armL` full-length daylight measures slightly negative at the elbow:
   daylightOf charges a tapered bar's far-end samples its fat-end effective
   radius, and the deltoid welds into the swollen shoulder by design. The
   honest checks (fuse < 0, forearm-vs-leg air > 0.28 m, human-arm daylight
   > 0.015) are pinned in the test with this tolerance documented.
5. The lab hero wanders (position + facing), so single-yaw captures are not
   reproducible between boots; full turntables + frame picking is the
   reliable capture flow (see above).
