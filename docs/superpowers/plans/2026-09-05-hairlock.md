## THE ONE JOB

A `.blob` modifier that turns ONE bent primitive into several distinct wavy
strands, for the case a chain of blobs does badly: a fringe, and the pointed
face-framing locks in front of the ears.

## LICENCE — NON-NEGOTIABLE

The technique is item 4 of
`Claude Notes/Blud/2026-08-24-selfie-girl-techniques.md`, whose source shader
**forbids reuse of the Work**. Do NOT open, read, search for, or port the
Selfie Girl shader. The technique is described in that note in our own words;
the legitimate implementation references are iq's own articles
(`iquilezles.org/distfunctions`, `/articles/sdfrepetition`, `/smin`).

## THE TECHNIQUE, IN OUR OWN WORDS

From the note: evaluate ONE bezier; in its cross-section plane, grid-repeat
with `round(q / cell)` over a small number of cells; give each cell an integer
id; add per-id jitter and a per-strand phase to the waviness along `t`. That
yields many visually distinct strands for one curve evaluation plus 2D
arithmetic, instead of one primitive per strand.

**`bend=` already gives you the bezier.** `coneBend` sweeps a capsule along a
quadratic Bezier with the radius lerping along the curve. This is a MODIFIER on
top of that, not a new primitive kind — the same call `box` and `ramp` made, so
it inherits `mirror`/`both`/`offset`/`core`/paint for free.

## SCOPE — READ THIS BEFORE DESIGNING

**This is for fringes and face-framing locks. It is NOT a replacement for hair
masses.** That is not a guess: the schoolgirl rerun was explicitly asked
whether it wanted this primitive, and answered in its own `.blob` header that
the two pointed side locks are "the ONE place strands would beat masses" while
the bob is better as masses. Two prims out of five would benefit.

So the acceptance case is a FRINGE or a pair of LOCKS that reads better than
the blobs it replaces — not a whole head of hair, and not a prim-count win
across a character.

## WHAT WENT WRONG LAST TIME A PRIMITIVE WAS ADDED

`radiusRamp` was built this way in September and its lessons are cheap to
reuse. Read `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-04-radius-ramp-design.md` at tag
`blobforge-experiment-2026-09-04` if you want the detail. The three that will
bite here:

1. **THE FIELD STOPS BEING A DISTANCE.** Domain repetition breaks the metric —
   `round(q/cell)` folds space, and the naive result is not a bound. Sphere
   tracing oversteps and punches holes. You must either bound the error or
   flag the region so the relaxed march steps plain there. `nearWound` in
   `march.wgsl.ts` is the existing pattern for "the field is not a distance
   bound here"; the cyclops's craters banding at relax 1.4 is what it prevents.
2. **BOUNDS MUST GROW.** A repeated prim occupies more volume than the one it
   came from. There are EXACTLY EIGHT outer-bound sites and they are already
   enumerated — every non-test call to `boxReach`/`rampReach`:
   `extent.ts`, `clusters.ts`, `validate.ts`, `pack.ts`, `rig-bind.ts`,
   `shell-hull-outer.ts`, and `fpv-view.ts` twice. `occluder-hull.ts` is an
   INNER hull: check whether strands shrink the inscribed sphere the way ramps
   did, and if so fix it there too — that is the one that produces see-through
   holes.
3. **`blob:rings` CANNOT MEASURE IT.** Report strand-carrying prims LOUDLY in
   their own "NOT measured" section, never folded into the quiet skipped list.
   A skipped bone is indistinguishable from a converged one, and that is
   exactly how the soldier shipped a pot-bellied egg past a clean report.

## NON-NEGOTIABLE: NOTHING MOVES

The modifier defaults to OFF, and a prim without it must render bit-identically
to today. Prove it: the suite has a snapshot-style sweep over every character
(`strandedOf`'s test is the pattern) — add or extend one so a regression is
caught rather than eyeballed.

## GATES

- **Unit:** absent modifier is a bit-exact no-op on an existing prim.
- **Unit:** the field is a valid bound — sample a strand-carrying prim on a
  dense grid and assert `|grad| <= 1 + 1e-3` by central differences, OR
  document why it cannot be and implement the plain-step flag instead.
- **Unit:** parameters outside their range are compile errors naming the line,
  not clamped values.
- **Render:** `blob:render-check` exit 0 on a strand fixture **at relax 1.4**,
  not only at 1.0. This is the gate that decides whether a plain-step flag is
  needed. Cite the cyclops crater bands as the reference artefact.
- **Perf:** record the step-count and frame-time delta. `radiusRamp` measured
  x1.803 against a naive prediction of 1.461 — budget worse than theory says.
- **Visual:** a fringe or a pair of face-framing locks that reads BETTER than
  the blobs it replaces. Shoot it and `Read` the frames. Closed is not the same
  as convincing.
- `npx vitest run src/lab/sdf-zombie/` green, `npx tsc --noEmit` clean.
