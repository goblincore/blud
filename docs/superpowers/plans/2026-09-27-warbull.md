# Warbull Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** implement task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax.

**Goal:** A playable cyber-minotaur: a flesh body with bolted-in machinery that
fires rocket volleys from an arm launcher, charges and gores up close, and goes
melee-only and enraged once the launcher is shot off.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-27-warbull-design.md` (read it first).

**Architecture:**

- **Body and kit:** the flesh is `warbull.blob` (the minotaur ×1.28). The hard
  parts are `warbull-kit.wam` plus a generated launcher `.glb`.
- **Pure modules** hold the new logic:
  - `status-lights.ts` for the LED pulse;
  - a rocket projectile step;
  - a warbull mind that layers the charge and disarm states over the soldier
    brain.

  The kit overlay and game-actor only read their output.
- **Damage:** `plate-armor.ts` and the soldier-family damage path are reused
  unchanged.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, three.js WebGPU + TSL, WGSL string modules, Vitest,
WAM (built on the owner's machine), a glTF-writing script for the prop.

## Rules for every task

- **Port-ready by construction:** logic in pure, renderer-free modules with their
  own tests. The renderer-facing module only reads that output. The simulation
  stays deterministic.
- **Targeted tests only**, plus `npx tsc --noEmit`.
- **Prove visual claims** with a number or a frame, and look at the images. This
  cloud container has no GPU: use CPU sdBody rasters and prop rasters here, and
  owner frames for the real look.
- **WAM cannot run in this container** (sandboxed as external code). The owner
  builds the kit with `scripts/build-wam-kit.sh warbull`. The fit test skips
  until the glTF exists.
- Extracted Blood assets are dev placeholders: never commit them.

## Task 1: body [x]

- **Files:**
  - `characters/warbull.blob`
  - `characters/warbull-blob.test.ts`
  - `public/assets/lab/faces/warbull-face.png`
  - the `warbull` entry in `character-registry.ts`
- **Changes:** the minotaur ×1.28; the painted prosthetic and cables dropped;
  the left horn sweep and left glow eye only; a hump behind the neck.
- **Pins:**
  - the scale is exact;
  - horn tip about 2.60 m, under `WALL_H` − 0.35;
  - soldier bone names;
  - no `metal`/`box` prims;
  - hump at least 4 cm proud;
  - fused, clear, and daylight no worse than the minotaur's.

## Task 2: kit [x] (authored and pre-flighted; the owner builds)

- **Files:** `characters/warbull-kit.wam`, `characters/warbull-kit.test.ts`
  (skips until built), a `kit:` line in the registry.
- **Parts, as spec'd:** steel horn and collar, optic, jaw brace, spine rack,
  reactor, knee cops and pistons, hooves, elbow socket, cable tubes.
- **Materials:** `chrome`, `iron`, `brass`, `lens`, `led`.
- **Every part meets flesh through a collar.** The fit test pins:
  - no vertex passes deeper than the collar tuck bound;
  - hooves on the ground;
  - bone heads within 1 mm;
  - every collar ring encloses the flesh it bites.

## Task 3: looks and lights [x]

- **Files:** `status-lights.ts` and its test (pure), plus `webgpu/kit-overlay.ts`:
  - `chrome` and `led` looks;
  - per-frame emissive from the pulse.
- **The pulse:** a function of mind state, time and plate state; it is
  deterministic.

## Task 4: launcher and rockets [x]

- **Files:**
  - `scripts/make-warbull-launcher.ts` writing
    `public/assets/lab/warbull-launcher.glb`;
  - a `launcher` carry in `carry.ts`: one-handed, the arm raised to point
    ahead, no fore hand;
  - `WARBULL_PROFILE` (soldier family, STOMP or MARCH, prop, `gunner.weapon:
    'rocket'`);
  - `ROCKET_TUNING` in `soldier-brain.ts`;
  - a pure rocket projectile module (step, hit test, detonate), handed to the
    dynamite blast path on detonation.
- **Pins:**
  - the muzzle points where he faces (walking and standing);
  - a volley is 3 rockets;
  - a rocket detonates on wall contact and at max range;
  - blast wounds reach actors in radius.

## Task 5: plates and disarm [x]

- **Files:**
  - `WARBULL_ARMOR` in `plate-armor.ts`;
  - `WARBULL_INJURY_TUNING`;
  - in game-actor, shedding the launcher plate hides the prop and drops a copy,
    and flips the mind to `disarmed`.
- **Pins:**
  - an absorbed launcher hit makes no wound;
  - shedding the launcher removes the ranged states;
  - blasts crack plates.

## Task 6: melee and the charge [x]

- **Files:** a pure `warbull-mind.ts` wrapping the soldier mind:
  - charge telegraph, charge, wall-stun, gore;
  - `disarmed` and enraged tuning.
- **Pins:** a charge runs a straight line; a wall ends it in a stun; gore fires
  only in reach; disarmed never fires.

## Task 7: game spawn

- **Files:** `SpawnKind` `'warbull'`, the level parser, `RoomDef.warbulls`, and
  one in the arena, as the Juggernaut did.
