# Soldier damage and reactions

Approved in conversation 2026-09-08. The user delegates unarmed animation choices.

Soldiers remain standing after either/both arms are lost. Loss of the support arm allows slower, less accurate one-handed shooting. Loss of the gun arm drops the gun and switches to close-range attacks: remaining-arm strike, or shoulder/body shove if both arms are missing. Leg failure can still down him.

Only a direct double-shotgun two-barrel head hit should instantly kill a healthy Soldier in ordinary firearm combat. Lesser head hits accumulate damage and reveal anatomy. Body shots progressively remove flesh; avoid a single stray head pellet deciding a volley. Retain explicit catastrophic/explosion outcomes where appropriate, without accidentally classifying a slug as the two-barrel shotgun.

Strong impacts interrupt firing, visibly throw shoulders/arms backward, and recover through a deliberate weapon reacquisition. Feet and locomotion stay grounded.

Wounds should have bloody irregular cavities and exposed bone like the Zombie. The face reveals an angular threatening skull with selective metal reinforcement. Armor impacts/shedding emit short sparks and fragments.

Use active SDF FPS only. Preserve Zombie behavior, renderer defaults, unrelated user edits, and dev-only asset restrictions. Verify focused damage/AI/motion tests, typecheck/build, and running WebGPU visuals. Keep changes on isolated codex/soldier-reactions for user playtest; no merge/push.
